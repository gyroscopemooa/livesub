How to Translate CHZZK Live Streams

Transtream turns browser audio into real-time multilingual subtitles so viewers can follow CHZZK live streams more easily.

CHZZK is a trademark of its respective owner. Transtream and live sub are independent services and are not affiliated with or endorsed by CHZZK.

What you need

  • A desktop browser with the live stream open in a tab
  • Transtream open in another tab or window
  • Your preferred subtitle language

Setup steps

  1. Open the CHZZK live stream in a desktop browser.
  2. Open Transtream and start a translation session.
  3. Select the CHZZK browser tab or system audio source.
  4. Choose the spoken language and subtitle language.
  5. Place the subtitle panel where it does not cover the player controls.

If captions do not appear

Confirm that the selected tab is producing audio, the tab is not muted, and the browser permission is active. Reconnect the source if the stream tab was reopened.

Important limitations

Transtream processes only browser audio made available to the session. It does not host, record, download, or redistribute the live stream.
