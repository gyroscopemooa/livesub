How to Translate SOOP Live Streams

Transtream translates browser audio into real-time subtitles, helping viewers follow SOOP live streams in a language they understand.

SOOP is a trademark of its respective owner. Transtream and live sub are independent services and are not affiliated with or endorsed by SOOP.

What you need

  • A desktop browser with the live stream open in a tab
  • Transtream open in another tab or window
  • Your preferred subtitle language

Setup steps

  1. Open the SOOP live stream in a desktop browser.
  2. Open Transtream and start a translation session.
  3. Select the browser tab or system audio source containing the stream.
  4. Choose the spoken language and subtitle language.
  5. Keep the stream audio unmuted and adjust the subtitle panel position.

If captions do not appear

Check the selected audio source, browser permissions, mute state, and spoken-language setting. Reconnect the source after reopening the stream tab.

Important limitations

Transtream processes only browser audio made available to the session. It does not host, record, download, or redistribute the live stream.
