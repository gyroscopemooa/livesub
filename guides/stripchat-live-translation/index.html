How to Translate Stripchat Live Streams

Transtream turns browser audio into real-time multilingual subtitles so viewers can follow a live stream more easily.

Stripchat is a trademark of its respective owner. Transtream and live sub are independent services and are not affiliated with or endorsed by Stripchat.

What you need

  • A desktop browser with the live stream open in a tab
  • Transtream open in another tab or window
  • Your preferred subtitle language

Setup steps

  1. Open the Stripchat live stream in your browser.
  2. Open Transtream and start a new translation session.
  3. Select the browser tab or system audio source that contains the stream.
  4. Choose the spoken language and the language for subtitles.
  5. Place the subtitle panel where it does not cover the stream controls.

If captions do not appear

Confirm that the stream tab is producing audio, the selected source is shared, and the spoken language is correct. Browser permissions may need to be granted again after a tab or window is reopened.

Important limitations

Transtream only processes browser audio made available to the session. It does not host, record, download, or redistribute the live stream.
