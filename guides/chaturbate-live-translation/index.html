How to Translate Chaturbate Live Streams

Transtream translates browser audio into real-time subtitles, helping viewers follow live streams in a language they understand.

Chaturbate is a trademark of its respective owner. Transtream and live sub are independent services and are not affiliated with or endorsed by Chaturbate.

What you need

  • A desktop browser with the live stream open in a tab
  • Transtream open in another tab or window
  • Your preferred subtitle language

Setup steps

  1. Open the Chaturbate live stream in your browser.
  2. Open Transtream and start a new translation session.
  3. Select the browser tab or system audio source that contains the stream.
  4. Choose the spoken language and the language for subtitles.
  5. Keep both tabs open and adjust the subtitle position or size for comfortable reading.

If captions do not appear

Check that the browser or system audio source is shared, the tab is not muted, and the selected spoken language matches the stream. If the stream changes language, restart the session with the new language selected.

Important limitations

Transtream only translates audio that your browser makes available to the session. It does not host, record, download, or redistribute the live stream.
